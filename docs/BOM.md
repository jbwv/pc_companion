# Bill of Materials

- **Board:** [Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B-C](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-lcd-3.5b.htm)
  — get the **"with case and OV5640 camera"** variant.
- **Stand:** [Amazon B07F8S18D5](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07F8S18D5) (any similar stand works fine — this is just what was used)
- **Cable:** A real USB A-to-C data cable, e.g. [Amazon B01GGKYN0A](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01GGKYN0A). Many cheap USB-C cables are charge-only and won't work for flashing — see the troubleshooting note in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) if the board isn't detected.
